# EtchBot Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 24, 2026

EtchBot is a companion app for the EtchBot drawing machine, developed by Jim Webb. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it doesn't collect any.

## Information we collect

EtchBot does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

## Photos and camera

When you take a photo or choose one from your library, EtchBot processes it entirely on your device to create a line drawing. Your photos are never uploaded to any server, and the app does not access any photos other than the ones you choose.

## Bluetooth

EtchBot uses Bluetooth only to connect to the EtchBot drawing machine and send it a drawing. What is sent is a list of line coordinates, not your photo. The machine keeps that drawing in its own memory until it is replaced by the next drawing or the machine is powered off.

## Data stored on your device

The app saves its settings and machine calibration values on your device. This data stays on your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

## TestFlight

If you install EtchBot through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to submit with the developer, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. That data is handled by Apple, not collected by EtchBot.

## Children

EtchBot does not collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to etchbot.app@icloud.com.
