# EtchBot Support

EtchBot turns your photos into single-line drawings and sends them over Bluetooth to the EtchBot drawing machine.

## Getting help

Email **etchbot.app@icloud.com** with your question. It helps to include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

## Common questions

**The app can't find my machine.**
Make sure the machine is plugged in and switched on, Bluetooth is on for your phone, and EtchBot has Bluetooth permission (Settings → EtchBot). The machine won't appear in iPhone's own Bluetooth settings list; connect from inside the EtchBot app.

**Do I need the machine to use the app?**
No. You can turn photos into line drawings and preview them without a machine. You need one to physically draw.

**Can I close the app while it's drawing?**
Yes. Once the drawing has finished transferring, the machine completes it on its own.

**Are my photos uploaded anywhere?**
No. All processing happens on your phone. See the [privacy policy](privacy.md).
